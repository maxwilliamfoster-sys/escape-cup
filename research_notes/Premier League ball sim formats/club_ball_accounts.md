# Football-club ball / marble simulation accounts (TikTok + YouTube Shorts)

Research date: 24-26 Nov 2026. All numbers come from third-party stats pages (vidIQ, TubeAI, TokGauge, TokRanks, AskNaveen, Socialcounts, Nexlev, Toklytics) or from video descriptions. TikTok's own pages returned no content when fetched, so no number here was read directly on tiktok.com. Each figure carries the date the stats page showed.

## Which accounts exist? (handle, platform, followers, views, dates)

### Takeaway
The football-club sim niche is led by **marble races** (Algodoo "Beat The Keeper" tournaments, real marble tracks, "marble football" match predictions), not by club-logo physics balls. I confirmed 10 relevant accounts. I found **no confirmed account that runs Premier League club crests in an escape-ring, HP-battle or rotating-ring format** (the Escape Cup style). That gap looks like an open opportunity, but it is unproven.

### Inferences
- The biggest club-sim numbers come from **multi-club long-form tournaments** on YouTube (Riley Marbles and Marble Race King, 0.4-1.4M views for a Premier League Beat The Keeper). On TikTok, **big-name multi-team or GOAT clips** do best (Marble Duel, 7-11M). Head-to-head Premier League match clips on TikTok did poorly (Marble Duel's Liverpool vs Tottenham got 37.7K; Monkeyfeesh's whole channel is under 10K subs).
- For TikTok, a Premier League format should probably have several clubs per video (for example all 20, or a "Big 6") or a strong hook. A plain one-match-per-video version seems weak.

## What exact format does each use? (arena, teams per video, win condition, length)

### Takeaway
The niche uses four formats: (1) "Beat The Keeper", where club marbles race to score 5 goals past a keeper in group rounds; (2) single-race league or season predictors; (3) marble "football" matches between two clubs; and (4) 2-5 club "duel" races. Escape-ring and HP-battle formats exist in the wider ball-sim niche (Earclacks, ring-escape tools) but I found none with Premier League clubs.

### Cited Findings
- **Beat The Keeper (Algodoo)**: "The main objective is to score 5 goals as soon as possible. First 2/4 clubs to do so, will advance into the next round... features 20 football clubs. Football Clubs that advance further, and also to which groups they will be assigned next, is completely random." It carries a disclaimer that the content is "randomly generated for entertainment purposes only". — [Socialcounts video page](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-video-live-view-count/AUOAksBBN9U)
  - Riley's Beat The Keeper Premier League videos run 21-23 minutes. Multi-league "100 Clubs" or "5 Leagues" races run about 50 minutes. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@rileymarbles/videos/)
  - Marble Race King's Champions League vs Europa League Beat The Keeper had 64 clubs (32 from each league) and ran about 30 minutes. — [p3no](https://www.p3no.no/tvp/champions-league-vs-europa-league-202223-beat-the-keeper-marble-race-marble-race-king_51b9c6558.html)
- **Fixture-by-fixture season (Monkeyfeesh)**: each real Premier League fixture is a two-marble race. The gameweek video (about 10 minutes, roughly 1 minute per match) has timestamps per match and ends with the "Latest League Table". Each match is also cut into a separate Short. — [NDA News](https://nz.nda.news/man-city-vs-southampton-st_247219/videos/marble-race-premier-league-gameweek-29-sn_6102636/)
- **Marble football (Damiko)**: marbles act as players on a pitch. Titles copy TV broadcasts: "MANCHESTER UNITED vs NEWCASTLE | PREMIER LEAGUE HIGHLIGHTS | MARBLE FOOTBALL |05/15/24| #espn #asmr", dated to the real fixture. Mostly Shorts, plus weekly full-league simulations. — [MyBizSpace](https://mybizspace.com/video/manchester-united-vs-newcastle-premier-league-highlights-marble-football-05-15-24-espn-asmr/); [Optell](https://www.optell.com/en/youtube-kol/damiko%2Bmarble%2Bshow-v2_c69c183adca863d21c2996e0/)
- **Duel races (Marble Duel)**: 2-5 entrants (clubs or star players), about 1:00-1:15 long. Captions are questions, such as "Which team wins this game?" and "Who's the best team of this trio?". — [TokGauge](https://tokgauge.com/@marble.duel3)
- **Real-track races (Boules et Billes)**: two marbles in club colours race through obstacles to a final hole; first in wins. — [Escrowhold](https://www.escrowhold.com/ucl-final-special-balls-predicts-winner-between-arsenal-and-paris-saint-germain/)
- **Marble Race League (Marble Race King)**: an ongoing league played as numbered "Days" across a season, with a "Complete Tournament" compilation at the end. — [TubeAI](https://tubeai.app/youtube/channels/marbleraceking)
- **Seasonal predictor (Ben Selfe)**: one race per division, with track stages themed as parts of a season ("referees, training ground and stadium"). Finishing order becomes the predicted table, including promotion and relegation spots. — [South Wales Argus](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/news/18767268.marble-race-predicts-newport-county-will-win-league-two/)

### Inferences
- An Escape Cup Premier League version (two club balls escaping rotating rings) would be a **new mechanic inside a proven theme**. The proven theme is club-vs-club "prediction" racing. The proven mechanic is ring escape, which so far is used for countries, colours and generic balls.

### Gaps
- I could not check video lengths for Damiko's Shorts or Monkeyfeesh's Shorts.

## Cadence and series structure

### Takeaway
Successful channels tie content to the **real football calendar**: a season opener when each league starts (August), the Champions League draw (September), and tournaments (World Cup, Club World Cup). Some also run matchday-by-matchday series that track the real fixtures.

### Cited Findings
- Riley Marbles posted Premier League Beat The Keeper (24 Aug), La Liga (29 Aug), Champions League Beat The Keeper (6 Sep) and a Champions League race (13 Sep) in 2026, all clustered at the start of the season. Its median gap between uploads is 12 days. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@rileymarbles/videos/)
- Riley's World Cup 2026 race (12 Jul 2026, the tournament period) got 6.4M views, about 13x its usual views. — [TubeAI](https://tubeai.app/youtube/channels/rileymarbles)
- Marble Race King uploads about 3.7 times a week. — [TubeAI](https://tubeai.app/youtube/channels/marbleraceking)
- Monkeyfeesh ran the full 38-gameweek 2024/25 season (for example Gameweek 15 in Dec 2024 and Gameweek 29 in Mar 2025), posting match Shorts plus a weekly compilation. — [Shotoe](https://cz.shotoe.com/west-ham-wolves-st_244532/videos/west-ham-vs-wolves-marble-race-premier-league-shorts-premierleague-westham-sn_5009657/)
- Damiko's channel bio promises "Daily football prediction Shorts" and "Weekly league simulations". — [Playboard](https://playboard.co/en/search?q=marble)
- Marble Duel posted roughly daily in mid-December 2025, then slowed to about 0.7 posts a week. — [TokGauge](https://tokgauge.com/@marble.duel3)

### Inferences
- A matchday-synced Premier League series would give Escape Cup a reliable weekly cycle: about 10 fixtures every weekend, a table update, and spikes around big derbies. Monkeyfeesh's tiny channel shows the series structure alone does not guarantee reach.

### Gaps
- I could not see how far a matchday series held its audience over the season (no per-gameweek view data for Monkeyfeesh).

## How they show standings and hook comments

### Takeaway
Standings appear as an end-of-video "latest league table" (fixture series) or as bracket and group progressions (Beat The Keeper). Comment hooks are almost always "who will win?" or "pick your marble", sometimes with "no switching" rules.

### Cited Findings
- Monkeyfeesh ends each gameweek video with a "Latest League Table" segment and asks "Who will you be supporting?" — [NDA News](https://nz.nda.news/man-city-vs-southampton-st_247219/videos/marble-race-premier-league-gameweek-29-sn_6102636/)
- Beat The Keeper uses group rounds where the first 2 of 4 clubs to score 5 goals advance, with random redraws for later groups. — [Socialcounts](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-video-live-view-count/AUOAksBBN9U)
- Marble Racing League caption: "Comment who you think will win NO SWITCHING". — [TokGauge](https://tokgauge.com/@marbleracingleague)
- Marble Duel captions: "Which team wins this game?", "Who's the winner for you?", "Rigged match?". The 11.3M and 11.2M clips drew 3,338 and 880 comments, plus 6,118 and 22,200 shares. — [TokGauge](https://tokgauge.com/@marble.duel3)
- The "prediction" framing spreads beyond the creator's own channel: Men in Blazers reposted Boules et Billes' PSG vs Arsenal race as a Champions League final prediction. — [Escrowhold](https://www.escrowhold.com/ucl-final-special-balls-predicts-winner-between-arsenal-and-paris-saint-germain/)
- Earlier precedent: Ben Selfe ran prediction competitions with prizes such as a funny drawing or poem for the winners. — [South Wales Argus](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/news/18767268.marble-race-predicts-newport-county-will-win-league-two/)

### Inferences
- Escape Cup could copy the "pick before it starts" hook: freeze the opening frame with both crests and "Comment your winner, no switching", then show a running table overlay.

## What makes top videos satisfying or viral

### Takeaway
The top performers combine **global mega-brands or rivalries** (Real Madrid vs Barcelona, Messi vs Ronaldo, the Champions League, the World Cup) with **timing around a real event**. Long tournaments with many entrants also do well on YouTube. Rivalry alone did not help mid-size Premier League pairings on TikTok.

### Cited Findings
- Marble Duel: clips with El Clásico or PSG and with Messi, Ronaldo, Dembélé or Yamal got 7.5-11.3M views. Premier League-only pairings (Liverpool vs Tottenham, Aston Villa vs Man Utd) got 17.8-37.7K. — [TokGauge](https://tokgauge.com/@marble.duel3)
- Event timing: Riley's World Cup 2026 race got 6.4M views. Marble Race King's World Cup Beat The Keeper got 5.5M. Marble Race King's 2026 World Cup Beat The Keeper Tournament got 929K. Champions League editions consistently beat Premier League editions on both channels (Riley's Champions League 2023/24 got 5.6M, against 0.38-0.59M for its Premier League videos). — [TubeAI Riley](https://tubeai.app/youtube/channels/rileymarbles); [TubeAI MRK](https://tubeai.app/youtube/channels/marbleraceking)
- The Arsenal vs PSG final-prediction marble race went viral right before the real final. — [Escrowhold](https://www.escrowhold.com/ucl-final-special-balls-predicts-winner-between-arsenal-and-paris-saint-germain/)
- Earclacks' HP-battle virality came from an escalating mechanic: each weapon "increases its damage/rotation speed every time it hits". Fans then clipped favourite characters with soundboard edits. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/weapon-ball-battles-earclacks)
- The tool makers' guidance on retention: make the ending uncertain (narrow gaps, spikes), use one clean bounce sound, post a series rather than one-offs, and end straight after the payoff. — [Satisfying Labs](https://satisfyinglabs.com/bouncing-ball-simulation)
- BounceReel says its Arena Clash format arranges "In four battles out of five, the winner holds fewer strings than a rival for most of the way and takes the lead only near the end", uses slow motion for the final eliminations, and adds a heartbeat sound at low counts. — [BounceReel](https://bouncereel.com/)

### Inferences
- For a Premier League Escape Cup, the strongest candidates are the Big 6, derbies (North London, Manchester, Merseyside), the title race, and the relegation battle. Late comebacks and slow-motion escapes add tension. Premier League vs Champions League crossover nights could also work.

### Gaps
- I could not see sound design or edits in the top videos directly. These points come from captions and tool descriptions, not from watching the videos.

## Crests, kit colours or names, and takedowns

### Takeaway
Practice is split. The marble channels mostly use **club names plus kit colours** on the marbles and real club names in titles. At least one commercial tool deliberately **avoids crests**. I found no public evidence of takedowns of club marble or ball sims, but absence of evidence is weak here.

### Cited Findings
- Boules et Billes used a plain red marble for Arsenal and blue for PSG (colours, no crest). — [Escrowhold](https://www.escrowhold.com/ucl-final-special-balls-predicts-winner-between-arsenal-and-paris-saint-germain/)
- Ben Selfe: "Each of the 92 teams are represented by a different marble matching their team's colours." — [South Wales Argus](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/news/18767268.marble-race-predicts-newport-county-will-win-league-two/)
- BounceReel: "Clubs and players are drawn in their colours, with their letters or numbers: no crests or photos... BounceReel is not affiliated with any club, league or player." — [BounceReel](https://bouncereel.com/)
- BounceMatch and bouncingballs.in openly let users upload team logos as ball skins. — [BounceMatch](https://bouncematch.com/); [bouncingballs.in](https://bouncingballs.in/)
- Beat The Keeper videos carry a "randomly generated for entertainment purposes only" disclaimer. — [Socialcounts](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-video-live-view-count/AUOAksBBN9U)
- Crest files are widely shared (SVG and PNG packs for all 20 2026-27 Premier League clubs), but the Premier League and its clubs own those badges as trademarks. — [FootyLogos](https://www.footylogos.com/competition/premier-league)

### Inferences
- The safest choice for Escape Cup: kit-colour balls with a three-letter club code (ARS, TOT, MCI), plus a "not affiliated" line. This matches the cautious end of current practice. Using real crests is common in the tools but carries trademark and takedown risk.

### Gaps
- I found no reports of TikTok or YouTube removals, Content ID claims or club cease-and-desist letters against marble or ball sims. I could not check any channel's strike history.
- I could not confirm whether Riley Marbles or Marble Race King use real crests on their Algodoo marbles (I could not view thumbnails or frames).
