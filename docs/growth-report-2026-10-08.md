## 3. Algorithm findings

**Confirmed (TikTok's own documentation):**
- For You ranking uses user interactions — "content you like, share, comment on, and watch in full or
  skip" — plus content information (sounds, hashtags, views, the country it was posted in) and user
  information (language, location, time zone, device). "User interactions, which may include the time
  spent watching a video, are generally weighted more heavily than others." ([TikTok Support](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content))
- Recommendations use similar-user patterns (collaborative filtering) and deliberately diversify; TikTok
  generally won't show two posts in a row from one creator.
- Search ranks mostly on how well the content matches the search term.
- New FYF rules (effective 24 Sep 2026): no "like-for-like", false incentives to follow, misleading claims;
  unoriginal/reused content ineligible; automation that "sends repetitive content" counts as spam.
  ([Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity))

**Strong evidence:**
- Small accounts average 4.2–4.4% engagement per view; views per video for 1–5K-follower accounts fell
  to ~350 in 2025 (−59% YoY). Escape Cup's ~260 median is *normal for its size*; the engagement isn't.
  ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok))
- Posting 2–5×/week gives the biggest lift; more posts add smaller gains (Buffer, 11.4M posts).
- My own data: every video's views arrive in one ~6-hour burst, consistent with batch testing.

**Speculation / creator theory (don't build on these):**
- Exact completion % thresholds ("70% to go viral"), "account warm-up", "200-view jail" as a penalty,
  private→public resets. Watch-time benchmarks by length come from vendors, not TikTok
  ([Retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/tiktok-retention-rate-benchmarks-2026)).

**What works, and why:**
1. **Many countries at once.** Every big country format puts 8–40 countries on screen. The viewer
   looks for *their* country — personal stake in the first second, and the reason to comment
   ("COMMENT YOUR COUNTRY", "Will YOUR country make it?").
2. **A visible countdown or elimination counter** ("8 LEFT", "within 50 seconds"). It turns watching
   into "how will it end?", which is what keeps people to the payoff.
3. **One-line hooks** that state the stakes: "Who will win?", "Will your country make it to the end?",
   "Can the ball escape within 50 seconds?" No sub-headers, no round info.
4. **Series and viewer input** ("Season 2 / Day 9", "Which countries next?") give people a reason to
   follow and come back.
5. **Length isn't the enemy:** the big hits run 25–110 s. They hold people because something is always
   at stake, not because they're short.
6. Captions are simple; hashtags are generic (#satisfying #simulation #bouncingball #countryballs).
   Everyone uses original physics sounds, not music.

**Gaps competitors are missing:** nobody combines the *ring-escape* mechanic with a *many-country*
field and a running *league/tournament*. @ballbattleroyale has no series; the marble races are
90–110 s and slow; the escape accounts have no national stakes.

## 5. Biggest bottleneck

| Rank | Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 CRITICAL | **No stake for the viewer.** Two random countries (Senegal v Italy) shown to a 91% UK audience — almost nobody cares who wins. | 5–7 s average watch on every video regardless of visuals; 0 comments; every successful country format uses many countries |
| 🔴 CRITICAL | **The first second doesn't pose a question the viewer wants answered.** "Which country escapes first?" + two small flags. | "most viewers stopped at 0:01"; the redesign didn't move watch time |
| 🟠 HIGH | **No reason to comment until it's too late.** "Comment your pick" only appears on the end card, which ~5% of viewers reach. | 0 comments; competitors show it from frame 1 |
| 🟠 HIGH | **Empty profile.** No display name, photo or bio. | 2 followers from 5,525 views |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | No visible countdown/progress besides a small score. | top escape videos all have one |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | Search discoverability (~0.1% search traffic). | captions don't use the terms people search ("country ball battle", "will your country") |
| 🟢 LOW | Posting times, frequency, sound, video quality | no evidence these are limiting; quality gate passes every video |
| 🟢 LOW | Shadowban / restriction | no evidence — 99% For You traffic |

**The 3 highest-leverage changes:**
1. **Many countries per video:** 8–16 country balls in the rings at once, so most viewers see their own or
   a rival's. The tournament continues (group heats → final) instead of 1v1 knockout.
2. **A first frame built around the stake:** "WHICH COUNTRY ESCAPES FIRST?" + a big live counter
   ("16 COUNTRIES · 1 WAY OUT", then "12 LEFT INSIDE") + "COMMENT YOUR COUNTRY 👇" on screen from 0:00.
3. **Measure and learn automatically:** daily Buffer metrics (built today — `analytics.py`), formats
   tested in alternation, decisions only after enough videos (rules below).

## 7. Retention

Without the Studio curves, the shape is inferred: average watch ≈ 5–6 s with "most stopped at 0:01"
means a large group leaves in the first 1–2 s and a small group watches to the end. The drop is at the
**premise**, not a slow middle. The fix is the premise (stake + counter), not further trimming —
the 18-second videos (25–32% watched) didn't hold people any better in seconds.

## 8. New video formula

| Step | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0.0 s | Headline "WHICH COUNTRY ESCAPES FIRST?"; 12–16 flag balls already moving; "16 COUNTRIES · 1 WAY OUT"; "COMMENT YOUR COUNTRY 👇" |
| Curiosity | 0–3 s | first ring breaks inside 1 s; viewers find their flag |
| Progression | 3–20 s | rings pop every ~1 s; live leader shown ("🇧🇷 leads"); counter of rings left |
| Pattern interrupts | every 4–6 s | combo breaks (several rings at once), a near-escape, the last ring turning red |
| Payoff | final 3 s | one country breaks out; podium (1st/2nd/3rd escaped) |
| Continuation | end card | "Group A winner → Final. Next: Group B" + "Your country wasn't in it? Comment it" (genuine question, no like/follow bait) |

Length 25–35 s (more balls = more happening; the niche holds 25–60 s fine).

## 10. Testing system

- **Primary metric:** % of video watched (Buffer average watch ÷ length). Secondary: engagement per
  view, comments per view, follows. Views alone are not a decision metric (test batches vary ±3×).
- **One change at a time**, alternating formats so time of day and day of week are balanced.
- **Decision rule:** at least **6 videos per variant** and a difference bigger than the spread of the
  baseline (±4 points in % watched; for engagement, at least 2× baseline). Otherwise: insufficient data —
  continue testing.
- **Order of tests:** (1) many-country format vs current 1v1; (2) 8 vs 16 countries; (3) hook wording;
  (4) countdown vs no countdown; (5) 25 s vs 40 s; (6) posting times.
- **Baseline to beat (12 videos):** 25.7% watched · 5.7 s average · 0.24% engagement · 0 comments ·
  median 263 views.

## 13. What to stop / keep

**Stop:** random 1v1 pairings; small round info on the first frame; asking for comments only at the end;
treating view count as the success metric.

**Keep:** the escape-ring mechanic (it's what the genre's biggest hits use), the quality gate, the soft
phone-audible sound, original physics (no AI label, no copyright risk), consistent posting via Buffer,
captions that name the countries (search).
